# Opening Announcement Draft

**Status:** Draft v3
**Date:** 2026-05-25
**Release date:** June 12, 2026 (30 days before opening)

The press release announcing Lark's opening. Will be sent to local press, food media, and our newsletter list.

## Headline

LARK, A NEW TASTING MENU RESTAURANT FROM CHEF AIYA TANAKA-BROWN, OPENS JULY 12 IN INNER SOUTHEAST PORTLAND

## Subhead

Twenty-eight seats. Twelve courses. Pacific Northwest food shaped by Japanese discipline.

## Lead

Aiya Tanaka-Brown, the chef whose work at Atelier Crenn, Single Thread, and Atomix earned her recognition for precision and seasonal intuition, will open Lark on Sunday, July 12, 2026, in inner Southeast Portland.

The restaurant will serve a single 12-course tasting menu, changing with the seasons, built around Pacific Northwest seafood and produce and shaped by Japanese technique. Lark will seat 28 diners across a counter, a communal table, and small tables. There is no à la carte. There is one menu and one price ($185 per person; pairings additional).

Lark is at 4127 SE Belmont Street, in a 1920s brick building most recently home to a beloved Portland bakery. The space, designed by Aiya and her partner Mateo Vasquez-Tanaka (sommelier and wine director), is small, warm, and unfussy.

## Quote (Aiya)

"The food at Lark is what's actually here in Oregon this week. I cook it the way I learned to cook from chefs who took it seriously: with discipline, without fuss. I hope the diners feel cared for."

## Quote (Mateo)

"The wine program at Lark is small and considered. We pour from Oregon, Burgundy, and Japan primarily — wines that respect the food and don't try to dominate it."

## Quote (Diane Park, investor and Portland restaurateur)

"Aiya is the kind of chef Portland's restaurant scene was waiting for. The food is uncompromising and the hospitality is real. I'm honored to be supporting Lark."

## The chef paragraph

Aiya Tanaka-Brown, 38, grew up between Seattle and Tokyo. Her mother, originally from Hokkaido, brought a Japanese kitchen discipline to a Seattle home that also held her father's American sensibility. She trained at Atelier Crenn in San Francisco, Single Thread in Healdsburg, and most recently Atomix in New York, where she worked for four years. She moved to Portland in late 2024 to begin building Lark.

## The space paragraph

Lark occupies a 1,850 square-foot space in inner Southeast Portland. The building, constructed in 1923, has held a bakery, a deli, a coffee shop, and another bakery before Lark. The renovation preserves the brick and the original windows; the interior is warm wood, soft light, and a counter that places diners directly across from the kitchen.

## The menu paragraph

The opening menu includes a smoked trout opening bite, geoduck with chrysanthemum vinegar, mushroom course with hen-of-the-woods, halibut, lamb shoulder, and a dessert built on Hokkaido milk. The menu will change with the seasons, with the first turn planned for mid-September.

## The team paragraph

Aiya is joined by sous chef Beto Castillo (formerly of Plaza Suite, NYC), pastry chef Lila Chen (formerly of Tartine Bakery, SF), wine director Mateo Vasquez-Tanaka, and general manager Caro Estefany Reyes (formerly of Castagna). The kitchen team includes line cooks Theo Aguilar, Inez Karpinski, and Jasper Mu. The front of house team includes servers Sienna, Kai, Erin, and Theo.

## Reservations

Reservations are available at lark-pdx.com via Tock. The opening months sold out within four days of release. New reservations open monthly, 60 days in advance.
