# Larsen Family Dairy and Cream

**Relationship:** Cream, butter, milk
**Located:** Tualatin Valley, OR
**Contact:** Sara Larsen
**First met:** 2025-10 at a Portland chef-meeting event
**Delivery cadence:** 3x weekly
**Status:** Workhorse dairy supplier; quality varies by item

## Background

Larsen is a mid-size organic dairy. They sell to several Portland restaurants. They are not the most boutique dairy in the area but they are reliable, organic, and the price-quality ratio is excellent for high-volume items.

Sara handles restaurant accounts.

## What we order

- **Milk** (regular and 2%, mostly for staff meal and the buttermilk ice cream Lila makes)
- **Heavy cream** for sauces (beurre blanc, brown butter, various)
- **Butter** (unsalted, European-style, 84% butterfat)
- **Buttermilk** (cultured, for the ice cream base and the bread starter)

Total monthly dairy spend with Larsen is approximately $1,100.

## Pricing

Standard wholesale pricing, no special arrangement. Their milk is $4/half-gallon, cream is $11/quart, butter is $7/lb.

## How we work

Standing order. They deliver Mon/Wed/Fri mornings. Quantities adjust based on the week's projection.

## What I'm reconsidering

The cream is the issue. Larsen's heavy cream is excellent but it skews slightly sweet, which is fighting the crème fraîche in the trout course (see Smoked Trout notes). I'm testing Garden Path cream (a different supplier) for that specific application.

If Garden Path wins for crème fraîche, we keep Larsen for everything else and source crème fraîche cream separately.

## Backup plans

For everyday dairy: Eberhard's Dairy (similar quality, slightly higher price). I have an account with them but don't use it actively.

## Personal note

Sara is professional and friendly. We are not particularly close. The relationship is straightforward commercial.

For the most flavor-critical items (the cheese course, the dessert ice cream base), I'm willing to use boutique sources. For the everyday workhorse items, Larsen is right.

## What I want long-term

Steady relationship. Larsen as our workhorse, with one or two boutique sources for specific applications.

## Note on butter

I've been using Larsen's European-style butter for everything but I'm starting to wonder if I should source a higher-fat cultured butter for specific finishing applications. Will test this against my baseline next month.

Lila is the one pushing on this — she wants better butter for the buckwheat shortbread. She's right that better butter would show in that application. The question is whether the cost lift is justified.

I think yes, for the shortbread only. Investigating sources.
