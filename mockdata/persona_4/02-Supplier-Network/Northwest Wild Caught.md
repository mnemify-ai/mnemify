# Northwest Wild Caught

**Relationship:** Wild ocean fish, primary supplier for fluke/halibut
**Located:** Astoria, OR
**Contact:** Diana (chef sales rep)
**First met:** 2025-10 at a chef summit
**Delivery cadence:** 2-3x weekly
**Status:** Important; quality varies by what came in

## Background

Northwest Wild Caught is a fishermen's cooperative based in Astoria. They aggregate the catch from a network of small-boat fishermen working the Oregon and Washington coasts. The catch is what the boats brought in that morning.

This means: high quality, very fresh, but inconsistent. Some days they have fluke. Some days they don't. Same with halibut, lingcod, rockfish, sablefish.

## What we order

Depends on what's coming in. For opening menu, the main interest is:

- **Fluke** for the opening bite (if Option C makes the menu)
- **Halibut** for course 6 (if halibut course makes the menu)
- **Sablefish (black cod)** for occasional special use
- **Lingcod** for staff meal

## Pricing

Market-based. Fluke is roughly $24/lb when available. Halibut is $32-38/lb depending on grade and season.

## How we work

Diana sends a morning "what came in" email by 8 AM each day. I respond by 9 AM with my order. Same-day delivery to the restaurant in the afternoon.

This is the most morning-of-the-day supplier we have. Beto has to be flexible — the prep doesn't start until we know what's coming.

## What worries me

The inconsistency is the entire model. Some weeks fluke is plentiful. Some weeks it's halibut. Some weeks the ocean has nothing I want.

We've built the menu to accommodate this — if the opener depends on fluke and the fluke isn't there, we have backup courses. But the operational flex is real and Beto is going to have to train the line for it.

## Backup plans

- For halibut: Lummi Island Wild
- For fluke: there isn't a great backup; if it's out, we pivot the opening bite to Option A (oyster) for the day
- For sablefish: I can get from a few other suppliers

## What Diana told me on a phone call last month

"The boats know your name. They're not setting aside fish for you — that's not how this works — but they know you're a customer who takes good care of the fish, so they bring their best to us."

I appreciate this. It's earned by paying on time and not being a difficult customer.
