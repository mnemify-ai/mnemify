# Pacific Pearl Sturgeon

**Relationship:** Sturgeon and sturgeon roe (caviar)
**Located:** Idaho (Hagerman Valley)
**Contact:** Marcus
**First met:** 2025-11 through Mateo's wine network
**Delivery cadence:** Bi-weekly
**Status:** Critical if halibut course pivots to sturgeon

## Background

Pacific Pearl raises white sturgeon in a closed-loop aquaculture operation in southern Idaho. They've been around about 25 years. Their main product is the caviar, but they also sell the smoked and fresh meat to a small list of restaurants.

The operation is sophisticated. Filtered spring water, controlled feed, careful handling. The fish quality is excellent.

## What we order

- **Smoked sturgeon** for the potential halibut-pivot course (we're testing sturgeon as the course 6 protein)
- **Sturgeon roe** in small quantities for the trout course (as the single drop of roe; though I might swap this for actual trout roe from Ron)
- **Fresh sturgeon** occasionally for special events

## Pricing

- Smoked sturgeon: $42/lb
- Caviar: $98/oz for the standard grade; premium is significantly higher
- Fresh sturgeon: $28/lb

The caviar pricing is what it is. We use small quantities so it works.

## How we work

Marcus emails me weekly. I order what I need. Standard refrigerated shipping, 1-2 day transit.

## What worries me

- Caviar prices have been climbing
- Sturgeon aquaculture is more reliable than wild but still has supply risk in heat waves
- The Idaho operation is far from us; transit issues can compromise product quality

## Backup plans

- For caviar/roe specifically, Ron's trout roe is a viable substitute and probably more on-brand for the restaurant
- For smoked fish in general, I have a backup with Hot Smoke Fish in Astoria

I haven't met Marcus in person yet. The relationship is by phone and email. It works for what we need.
