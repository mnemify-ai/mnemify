# Mutual Trading — Japanese Pantry

**Relationship:** Japanese ingredients wholesaler
**Located:** Los Angeles (West Coast hub)
**Contact:** Kenji at the chef desk
**First met:** They were my supplier at Atomix; brought the account with me
**Delivery cadence:** Weekly, shipped overnight
**Status:** Essential for hanakatsuo, kombu, certain rice products

## Background

Mutual Trading is the largest Japanese-product distributor in the US. They serve thousands of restaurants. They're a commercial relationship — no romance about it — but they're reliable.

For specialty items (high-grade hanakatsuo, specific kombu varieties, certain sake-adjacent products), they have access I don't have from other sources.

## What we order

- Rishiri kombu (base dashi)
- Ma-konbu (lamb glaze dashi)
- Hanakatsuo (bonito shavings) — workhorse quality, fresh weekly
- Premium sake lees (kasu) when we need it
- Certain rice varieties (koshihikari for staff meal; ojiya rice for occasional use)
- Sansho peppercorns (whole, fresh-ground in house)
- Yuzu kosho (occasionally; mostly we make our own when yuzu is in season)
- Various small finishing items

Total monthly order is roughly $1,400-1,800 depending on season and menu.

## How we work

Kenji is great. He flags new arrivals that might interest me, gives me a heads up on price changes, and finds substitutes when something I want is out of stock.

I send orders by Friday, delivery Wednesday the following week (shipped overnight Tuesday).

## What worries me

- Supply chain disruptions to Japan have been more frequent in recent years (typhoons, shipping delays). For workhorse items, Mutual usually has buffer stock; for specialty items, sometimes we wait 4-6 weeks for replenishment.
- Price volatility on premium items (matsutake, certain sake lees) is significant.

## Backup plans

- For kombu specifically, I have a Vancouver BC contact (small specialty shop) and a Seattle import shop. Both have higher per-unit prices but accessible if needed.
- For hanakatsuo, the Vancouver supplier (V3 test in the Dashi log) is an alternative for special use, not workhorse.

## What Kenji told me when I left Atomix

"Don't forget us when you open. New chefs forget where the dashi comes from."

I have not forgotten. Mutual Trading is in the order rotation every week.

## What I want long-term

Steady relationship. They are not a romantic supplier — they're a wholesale operation. But the reliability is everything. As long as the hanakatsuo is consistent, this relationship works.
