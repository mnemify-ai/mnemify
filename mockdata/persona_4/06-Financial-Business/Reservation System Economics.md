# Reservation System Economics

**Status:** Live on Tock; tested
**Date:** 2026-05-18

The economics of how we take and manage reservations.

## Platform

Tock (not Resy, not OpenTable).

Tock works for tasting menu restaurants because it supports prepaid deposits. Resy and OpenTable are weaker at prepaid models.

### Cost
- $649/month base
- 2% transaction fee on prepaid bookings

Annual: ~$8,200 platform + ~$24,000 in transaction fees on ~$1.2M revenue = ~$32K/year.

This is more than OpenTable would be. The deposit model justifies it.

## The deposit model

- Each reservation requires a $50 per cover deposit (refundable up to 48h before)
- 48-24h before: $50 deposit retained if cancelled
- Within 24h or no-show: full menu price billed ($185 per cover)

This is the no-show prevention model. Standard for tasting menus.

## Why deposits matter

A tasting menu kitchen preps for a specific number of covers. A no-show means food prepped for nothing. That food was 28% of the would-be revenue.

At 28 seats, one no-show is ~$185 in food cost and revenue gone.

Pre-pandemic, no-shows at tasting menus ran 5-8%. Post-pandemic, they're higher (10-15%) if you don't take deposits.

Deposits reduce no-shows to under 2%. The math: deposits paid for the platform many times over.

## How diners respond to deposits

Most accept it. A few balk. The balk rate has been about 10% during the soft-opening reservation period.

When someone balks, Caro explains:
- Tasting menus require us to prep a specific menu for them
- Deposits ensure we can plan
- The deposit is fully credited toward their meal cost
- We are happy to refund if they cancel up to 48h before

90% accept after the explanation. 10% don't book. Fine.

## Reservation timing

- Reservations open 60 days in advance (rolling)
- Peak booking: 14-21 days out
- Same-day reservations: rare; we keep 2 seats for walk-ins/last-minute

We released the first month of reservations 60 days before opening (May 12 for July 12). Sold out in 4 days. Second month released May 27.

## Waitlist

We have a waitlist for sold-out nights. ~120 people on it for the opening month.

We notify the waitlist when:
- A cancellation opens a seat (within 24h, free seat goes to top of list)
- A new reservation block opens (60 days in advance)

## Cancellations and reschedules

Process for cancellations:
- 48h+ before: full refund
- 24-48h before: $50/cover retained
- 0-24h before: $185/cover billed

We are flexible in true emergencies (medical, family). Caro handles these case-by-case.

We are not flexible in non-emergency reschedules. The diner who reschedules at 11am the day of has affected our prep and team.

## Group reservations

Tables of 6 or more:
- Require explicit communication with Caro before booking
- Full menu price ($185 × covers) as deposit
- Refundable up to 7 days before

We can take groups of up to 8 (the communal table).

## VIP and friends-of-the-house

We have a small list (~20 people) who can text Caro or me directly for reservations. Diane, Mateo's parents, my mother, a small circle. They get priority booking.

We do not offer comps but we do bend the deposit rule for them. They are good for it.

## The hardest reservations to handle

- Anniversary requests where the specific date is the anniversary (high emotional stakes)
- Out-of-town guests with constrained travel windows
- Proposals (we coordinate carefully)
- Diners with very specific dietary requirements who need pre-confirmation
- Press requests (Caro handles; we don't comp press but we accommodate scheduling)

## What I won't do

- Sell off-menu "VIP" experiences for higher prices
- Offer hidden access to insiders that the public doesn't know about
- Charge differential prices based on demand
- Compromise the deposit policy for "important" people

We are one menu, one price, one experience. The reservation system reflects that.

## Personal note

Reservation policies are surprisingly emotional for diners. They will accept a $185 menu price but balk at a $50 deposit. They will accept a 90-day booking window for a wedding but get angry at a 60-day window for a meal.

We are firm without being cold. Caro is the master of this. She can deliver a hard policy as kind hospitality.

I keep learning from her.
