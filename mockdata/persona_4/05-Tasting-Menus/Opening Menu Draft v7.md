# Opening Menu Draft — v7

**Status:** Current working draft
**Date:** 2026-05-27
**Previous version:** v6 (2026-05-15)

The 12-course tasting menu we plan to open with. Locked-in-pencil, not locked-in-ink.

## The arc

1. **Opening Bite** — Smoked trout, salt-cured egg yolk, nori cracker
2. **Geoduck and Chrysanthemum** — Geoduck sashimi, chrysanthemum vinegar, finger lime
3. **Bread** — Sourdough, cultured butter, smoked seaweed salt
4. **Soup** — Hot dashi, matsutake, yuzu kosho
5. **Salad** — Spring greens, sea bean, white miso vinaigrette
6. **Cold Trout** — Cured trout, sansho cream, pickled fennel
7. **Mushroom Course** — Hen-of-the-woods, brown butter, charred leek
8. **Halibut** — Halibut, dashi-poached celery root, brown butter dashi
9. **Lamb** — Lamb shoulder, fermented black garlic, savory plum
10. **Cheese** — Ancient Heritage Adelle, honey from Bee Local, bee pollen tuile
11. **Pre-Dessert** — Yuzu sorbet, shiso, white sesame crunch
12. **Dessert** — Hokkaido milk, brown butter cake, miso caramel, smoked salt

## What's new in v7

Changed from v6:
- Swapped sturgeon course (v6) back to halibut (v7). Sturgeon supplier issue still unresolved. Halibut from Northwest Wild Caught is more reliable.
- Soup moved earlier (was course 7 in v6). I think hot soup early grounds the menu after the cold bites.
- Cheese moved later (was course 9). Better arc into dessert.

## The pacing concerns

The arc cold-to-warm-to-rich-to-sweet is correct. But:

- Courses 6-7 (cold trout to mushroom) is a temperature break that needs management. Cold to hot in one course shift. We need the mushroom course to land HOT.
- Course 9 to 10 (lamb to cheese) is the heaviest stretch. Lila's working on the cheese plate being lighter than traditional.
- Course 11 (pre-dessert) is the palate cleanser. Has to be sharp, bright, short.

## The signature

When people leave Lark, I want them to remember the smoked trout opening bite and the lamb course.

The opening bite is the first impression. The lamb is the heaviest moment.

Everything else is the arc between.
