# 2026-05-22 — The opening date is real

8 weeks to opening. The first time I have written that and felt it in my chest.

Today we got the final piece of equipment delivered (the dashi cooker — custom, from Tokyo). Beto and I unpacked it together in the empty kitchen. It is beautiful. It works. The kitchen is now complete.

The kitchen being complete is a milestone. I want to mark it.

We poured a small sake (Tedorigawa Yamahai) and toasted. Beto said: "we are ready."

I said: "we are getting ready."

He said: "we are ready."

He was right. The kitchen is ready. The team is mostly ready. The supplier network is in place. The menu is in pencil and the pencil is good.

What's not ready:
- The team has not cooked the full menu under service pressure
- The FOH has not run a real night
- The diner-experience integration has not been tested at full pace
- The kitchen-FOH coordination is theoretical
- The systems (POS, reservation, payroll) are tested but not stressed

These will get fixed in the soft opening. That is what the soft opening is for.

What is real now: the date is July 12. It is not theoretical. The reservation list is set. Mateo's family is flying in. My mother is flying in. Diane is hosting an investors' table at the opening week.

The opening is a thing that will happen whether or not we are ready.

I am OK with this. The slow accumulation of preparation eventually becomes presentation. There is no perfect moment.

What I think about tonight: how I will feel on July 12 at 5pm, 60 minutes before doors open.

I think I will feel calm. I think I will feel small. I think I will feel ready.

I do not think I will feel proud. The pride comes later, if at all. Right at 5pm on July 12 I will just be checking the prep list and the mise en place and the temperature on the walk-in.

That is enough.

The opening date is real. The work is the only thing.

— Aiya
