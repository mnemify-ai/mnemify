# 2026-05-25 — On the team

The team is everything. I want to write down what I think of each of them right now, before opening, before the reality of service forces me to think of them through a different lens.

**Beto** (sous chef). Beto is the right person. He is calm without being passive. He sees what I see and a few things I miss. He pushes back when he should. He defers when he should. He has held my hand through three breakdowns about the menu in the past three months. The kitchen is his as much as mine. I would not be here without him.

**Caro** (GM). Caro is the spine of the restaurant. She is doing the work I cannot do — the FOH building, the system setup, the diner-care discipline. She is also better at the people work than I am. I owe her more than I can pay. I am thinking about how to structure that recognition.

**Mateo** (partner; wine director). I cannot write objectively about Mateo. He is the love of my life and the wine director. The fact that he is both is the joy and the risk of all of this. We are good. We are going to be tested.

**Lila** (pastry). Lila is younger than the others (28) but she has the maturity of someone older. Her pastry palate is precise. She makes everyone laugh at family meal. She is the team's morale center as much as its dessert center.

**Theo** (line cook). Theo is the youngest in the kitchen. He is going to be a great chef. I am responsible for not breaking him in the first year.

**Inez** (line cook). Inez is the steady one. She has done this work in 8 other kitchens. She brings calm. She has not yet brought all of herself; I want her to. I am working on creating the conditions for her to fully arrive.

**Jasper** (line cook). Jasper is talented and sensitive. The kitchen has not been kind to sensitive cooks historically. I want to be the kitchen that is.

**Sienna** (counter server). Sienna is going to surprise us. She is quiet in training but I see her processing everything. When the counter goes live I think she will be a captain-track within a year.

**Kai** (communal server). Kai's polish is real and a challenge. His warmth is the project. Caro is working on it.

**Erin** (server, future captain). Erin is the closest to a finished captain. She knows what she is doing. She is also generous with the new servers in a way that doesn't read as competing for the captain spot. She will be the captain.

**Floor Theo** (server). Floor Theo (different from Kitchen Theo) is great with regulars. He has the memory of a sommelier for diner preferences. Diane noticed it on the first private dinner.

**Lia** (host). Lia is part-time and good at her job. She is going to grad school. She will leave us within 18 months. That is OK; we will replace her.

**Diane** (investor; mentor). Diane is the reason any of this is happening at the scale it is. I do not have the words.

This is the team. There are 12 of them (plus Diane).

I want to remember today that I trust each of them. The trust is real. The opening will test it. I want to come back to this list in October and see what I think then.

— Aiya
