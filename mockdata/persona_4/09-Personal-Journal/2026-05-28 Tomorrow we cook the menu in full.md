# 2026-05-28 — Tomorrow we cook the menu in full

Tomorrow morning Beto and I will cook the full 12-course menu for 8 staff. Family meal style but at full menu speed. The first time we cook the full menu in the actual kitchen, with the actual equipment.

I am writing tonight because tomorrow will be a long day and I want to be honest now about where I am.

I am scared. The scared is not catastrophic. It is the right amount of scared for tomorrow.

The scared is: what if the menu doesn't hold together at full pace? What if courses 4 and 5 collide? What if the lamb course is overshadowed by the mushroom course? What if my technique is rusty after 6 months of build-out work and not enough cooking?

The honest answer to all of these: we will see tomorrow. The menu either holds or it doesn't. We will adjust based on what we see.

I am excited. The excited is bigger than the scared.

The kitchen is mine. The team is mine. The menu is mine. Tomorrow is the first day all of those things will be true at the same time in the actual space.

I have been waiting for tomorrow since I was 22.

What I want to do tomorrow well:
- Cook calmly
- Listen to the team
- Watch the timing
- Taste each course before service
- Take notes
- Not panic

What I want to avoid tomorrow:
- Trying to do it all myself
- Overcorrecting in real time
- Telling Beto how to do his job
- Forgetting to eat
- Forgetting to drink water

What I want to remember:
- This is one day. Tomorrow is one day. The opening is not until July 12. We have weeks to fix what doesn't work tomorrow.
- The team is good. They will surprise me.
- The menu has been tested. It will mostly hold.
- The food will not be perfect. That is OK. Perfection is the wrong goal. The right goal is excellent, learnable, repeatable.

What I want from the day after tomorrow:
- A list of 10-15 things we need to adjust
- The confidence that the menu is right
- A sense of where the kitchen needs to grow
- Sleep

I am going to bed. Mateo is already asleep. The kitchen tomorrow morning will be cold and empty until Beto arrives. I will arrive first. I will turn on the lights. I will put on water for tea. I will check the walk-in.

The kitchen tomorrow will smell like dashi by 9am. The first stock of the day.

Tomorrow we cook the menu in full.

— Aiya
