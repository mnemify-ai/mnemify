# POS and Reservation System

**Status:** Selected and being implemented
**Date:** 2026-05-15
**Owner:** Caro

## POS

We chose **Toast** after considering Square and Lightspeed. Reasons:
- Strong integration with reservations
- Good kitchen display system
- Reasonable pricing for our size
- Reliable support

Cost: $69/month per terminal + 2.49% + $0.15 per transaction.

## Reservation system

We chose **Tock** for reservations. Reasons:
- Designed for prix fixe and tasting menus
- Allows pre-payment (which we want)
- Strong support for our format
- Integration with marketing tools

Cost: $199/month flat, plus a small per-cover fee.

## Pre-payment policy

All reservations require pre-payment of the full menu price ($295) at the time of booking. This is standard for tasting menus and:

- Eliminates no-shows almost entirely
- Improves cash flow
- Self-selects for diners committed to the experience

Pairing is not pre-paid; it's added at the restaurant.

## Cancellation policy

- More than 72 hours: full refund
- 24-72 hours: 50% refund
- Less than 24 hours: no refund

Exceptions for genuine emergencies, evaluated case by case.

## Reservation cadence

Reservations open 60 days in advance. New reservations are released every Monday at 10 AM for the corresponding date 60 days out.

This rolling release builds anticipation and rewards regular booking.

## What worries me

- Tech failures during service. We have backup paper systems.
- Pre-payment system can be a friction for some guests. Worth it.
- The 60-day window means we have to forecast carefully.

## Personal note

This is operational plumbing. It needs to work. It does not need to be exciting.
