# Cleaning and Sanitation Protocols

**Status:** Documented; being trained
**Date:** 2026-05-21
**Owner:** Beto (kitchen), Caro (FOH)

## Daily cleaning

### Kitchen — end of service

- Hot line: wiped down with sanitizer, range cleaned, flat top scraped and seasoned
- Cold line: cleared, wiped, refrigeration reset
- Walk-in: floor mopped, surfaces wiped
- Dish station: full deep clean of dishwasher, sinks scrubbed
- Hood filters: removed and sent to dishwasher (every 3rd day)
- Floor: swept and mopped

### Kitchen — pre-service

- All surfaces sanitized
- Sanitizer buckets refreshed (one per station)
- Hand sinks stocked and tested
- Temperature logs filled out (walk-in, refrigeration units)

### FOH — end of service

- Tables wiped and reset
- Floor vacuumed
- Bar cleaned (Mateo)
- Bathrooms cleaned and stocked

## Weekly deep cleans

- Walk-in: full empty, deep clean, restock (Sunday mornings)
- Hood: professional cleaning service every 90 days
- Kitchen floor: deep degrease (Sunday)
- Equipment maintenance: scheduled service for major equipment

## Sanitation training

All staff complete food handler certification before service. Beto runs a 30-minute sanitation refresher in family meal once a week for the first month.

## Pest control

We have a contract with a commercial pest control service. Monthly inspection and treatment. Records kept in the binder.

The previous tenant in this space had a roach problem. We did a full pre-occupancy treatment and have been clean since.

## Trash and recycling

- Front-of-house trash: emptied during service as needed
- Kitchen trash: continuous emptying, full discard at end of service
- Recycling: separated by type, picked up Tuesdays and Fridays
- Compost: picked up daily by our compost service (Republic Services compostable program)

## Personal note

This document is boring. It is also the difference between a restaurant that operates cleanly and one that doesn't. Beto trains on this with the same rigor as he trains on cooking technique.

The standard at Lark is: a health inspector who walks in unannounced finds nothing to write up. We will live this standard from day one.
