# HVAC and Acoustics Resolution

**Status:** In progress
**Date:** 2026-05-26

## The problem

The kitchen hood exhaust is louder than I want when on high. The dining room can hear it during quieter conversation moments. This affects the chef's counter most, then the back two-top.

Westside Mechanical installed the original hood. They claim it's within spec. It's within commercial spec; it's not within the experiential spec I want.

## What we're trying

- **Baffle revision** in the hood plenum to reduce turbulence noise. $1,800 estimated. Caro is sourcing.
- **Acoustic panels** in the dining room — recessed into the ceiling in three locations. Custom-made, fabric-wrapped. $4,400 estimated. We're going to do this regardless of the hood issue because the room is too live.
- **Operating the hood at 80% during service** rather than 100%, except during peak smoke moments. This is a procedural change and costs nothing.

## What I'm worried about

If after all the fixes the hood is still audible during quiet moments, we may need to redesign the duct routing. That's a major construction job we cannot do before opening.

Worst-case workaround: pre-condition the hood (run high before service, drop to low during quiet moments, ramp up during plating).

## Timeline

- Baffle revision: this week
- Acoustic panels: ordered, install 2026-06-15
- Procedural changes: in place for soft opening 3

## What Caro found

She found three other Portland restaurants with the same hood specification and called them. Two reported similar acoustic issues. One had solved it with the baffle revision we're trying.

This is the kind of research a good operations partner does. I am grateful for Caro.
