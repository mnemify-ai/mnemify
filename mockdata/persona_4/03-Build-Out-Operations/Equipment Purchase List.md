## Total equipment investment

Major equipment: $130,600 (rounded)

This is high but in line with what a serious tasting menu restaurant requires. Diane reviewed and signed off.

## Equipment that didn't make the cut

- **Combi oven.** I wanted one badly. They start at $12k. The convection oven + salamander + plancha combination covers what we need. Saved $12k.
- **Tilt skillet.** Useful for large-batch braises but not for our format. Saved $8k.
- **Dedicated charcoal grill.** I wanted to do certain things over coals. Code restrictions on the building prevented this. Saved $6k.

## Maintenance budget

Estimated $400-600/month for routine equipment maintenance. Major repairs as they come.

## Lifecycle expectations

Most heavy equipment should last 8-12 years with proper maintenance. The dishwasher is the wear item — typically 5-7 years.

## Personal note

I have been thinking about this equipment for years. Every choice is a balance between what I want and what the budget allows. Diane forced me to cut several wish-list items (combi oven especially). She was right.

The kitchen we have is the kitchen we'll cook in. It's good. It's not the world's most luxurious kitchen, but it's right-sized for what we're trying to do.

Now we cook.
