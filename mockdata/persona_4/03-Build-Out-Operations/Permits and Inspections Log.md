## What worries me

The OLCC license is the biggest risk. Their timing is opaque and slipping their deadline by 2-3 weeks would push us into August opening with no wine, which would be terrible.

The HVAC commissioning is the second-biggest risk. The contractor (Westside Mechanical) has been generally good but the schedule has slipped twice. If they slip again, we miss the building inspection date.

## Process learnings

- Permits take longer than your contractor tells you. Plan for it.
- Multiple permits depend on each other (building before fire before health). Bottleneck management is real.
- Personal calls to inspectors and license officers are more effective than emails. Caro has been doing this masterfully.
