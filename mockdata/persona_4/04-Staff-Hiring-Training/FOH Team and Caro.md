## Training program

We've been running a 5-week training program. Weeks 1-3 happened during build-out final stages; weeks 4-5 happen during the soft openings.

Topics covered:
- The menu (every course, every component, every technique)
- The wines (Mateo)
- The choreography (Caro)
- The diner-care moments (Caro)
- Handling difficult situations
- Recovery from service mistakes
- Cleaning and reset protocols

Each server has been through every position multiple times.

## What I'm watching

- Counter service stress. The counter is the hardest position because the diner is watching the kitchen and asking questions. Sienna is calm but new at this dynamic.
- Kai's polish edge. He's so polished that he can read cold to some diners. We're working on warmth.
- Erin's potential as captain. If she's ready by August, we promote her and add a junior server.

## Tip pooling

We pool all tips and distribute by hours worked. 60% to FOH, 30% to BOH, 10% to management.

This is a fair distribution. Some staff would prefer individual tipping (servers especially). We hire people who buy into the pooled model.
