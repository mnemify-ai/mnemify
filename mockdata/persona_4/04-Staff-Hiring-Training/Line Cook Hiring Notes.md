## Still open: Pastry/Garde Manger crossover position

The fourth line cook position is a hybrid — primarily working with Lila on pastry/cold prep, also covering garde manger during service. Hard to fill because we need pastry skills plus line cook stamina.

Two candidates so far. Neither has been right.

- **Sarah** (interviewed 2026-05-15): Strong pastry. Weak line skills. Would need 6 months of training before being useful at service.
- **Marco** (interviewed 2026-05-22): Strong line skills. Weak pastry. The reverse problem.

We need both. I haven't found anyone who has both. Lila is interviewing again next week.

## What I want in this last hire

- Real pastry chops (Lila assesses this)
- Speed and consistency at the line
- Willingness to play a hybrid role
- Eager to grow with the restaurant

If we don't find someone by mid-June, we open with Lila and Theo splitting the role. Not ideal. Workable.

## My hiring philosophy

- Hire for trajectory, not credentials
- Trial days are non-negotiable
- Reference checks are real, not perfunctory
- Pay above market for the role
- Be honest about the demands of the job

Two people I interviewed and didn't hire told me afterward they appreciated my honesty about the difficulty of the role. Both said most chefs they'd interviewed with had soft-pedaled the demands.

I do not soft-pedal. Lark is going to be a hard kitchen. If you can't handle that, please don't take the job.

## Compensation for line cooks

- $24-28/hour based on experience
- Health insurance after 90 days
- Profit sharing after year 1
- 10 days PTO
- Meals during shift

This is high for Portland. Diane raised her eyebrow at the rate. I told her: the cost of replacing someone is much higher than paying them well.
