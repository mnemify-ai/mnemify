# BSA/AML Audit Prep — June 2026

**Audit date:** 2026-06-08 (Monday) through 2026-06-12 (Friday)
**Auditor:** External firm (engaged annually)
**Internal lead:** Maya, with compliance counsel
**Status:** Prep in flight
**Date:** 2026-05-22

## What this audit is

Annual independent review of our BSA/AML program. Required by our sponsor bank (Pillar) and by regulatory expectations. Auditor reviews:
- Customer identification program (CIP)
- Customer due diligence (CDD)
- Suspicious activity monitoring
- SAR filing process
- OFAC sanctions screening
- Records retention
- Training program

Not an examination by a regulator. Our auditor reports to us and to Pillar Bank.

## What I want from this audit

- **Zero material findings.** Realistic: 1-2 minor findings is normal. Material findings = problem.
- **Cleanlier than last year.** Last year we had 3 minor findings. This year I want 1 or zero.
- **Documentation ready, not improvised.** Auditors notice when policies are written the night before.

## What's improved since last audit

- KYC vendor migration (Persona → Alloy) underway. Won't affect this audit but shows direction.
- SAR filing process formalized in Q4 2025. Last audit found we had ad-hoc process.
- Enhanced due diligence (EDD) thresholds defined. Last audit found these were undocumented.
- OFAC screening cadence formalized (real-time at signup, daily for existing users). Was monthly batch last year.
- Training program updated and annualized. All employees trained Q1 2026.

## What's still a gap

- **Transaction monitoring rules.** We have rules but they're under-tuned. Too many false positives. Auditor may flag if they review actual alert quality.
- **Investing product compliance is partial.** Investing launches in Q3, after this audit. Auditor won't see the new product fully. But they may ask about readiness.
- **Customer risk scoring.** We have a basic scoring system. Not as sophisticated as larger fintech competitors. May or may not be a finding.

## Auditor will ask

Based on last year's audit:

- Show me your CIP procedures and a sample of recently-verified customers
- Show me your CDD/EDD documentation, with examples of EDD applied
- Show me SARs filed in the past 12 months
- Walk me through how a suspicious transaction would surface
- Show me OFAC screening logs
- Show me how you handle a customer who can't be verified
- Show me training completion records
- Show me your most recent risk assessment

All of these are ready. Compliance binder is in OneTrust.

## Risks

- **Transaction monitoring finding.** Likely. Mitigation: have a plan for tuning, present it, get auditor sign-off on the plan as remediation.
- **Investing readiness question.** Probable. Mitigation: pre-prepared narrative — we know the gaps, we have a plan, the launch isn't until Q3.
- **A team member says something off-script.** Possible. Mitigation: prep document for everyone likely to be interviewed.
- **An unknown issue surfaces.** Always possible. Mitigation: maintain calm, document, address, don't argue.
