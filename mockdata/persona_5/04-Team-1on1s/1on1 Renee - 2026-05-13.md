**Investing feature scope decisions.** She wants to add a "performance projection" widget to the investing tab. Shows users projected portfolio value at 5/10/20 years based on current contributions.

I pushed back hard. Projection widgets in regulated financial products are minefields. They have to comply with SEC marketing rules. They're easy to get wrong. They invite questions during regulatory review.

Renee acknowledged the risk but argued: it's the most-requested feature from the customer interviews she's been running. Users don't understand what investing means without a forward-looking number.
