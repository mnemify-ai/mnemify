# 1:1 with Lin — 2026-05-15

**Time:** 30 min, Friday 3 PM
**Format:** Weekly recurring

## What Lin brought up

**Investing feature design — feeling stretched.** She's the only designer. Investing is a huge surface. She's been pushing on it for 6 weeks and the design system isn't keeping up.

Her ask: bring in a contract designer for 3 months to handle the routine UI work while she focuses on investing-specific design.

I agreed. We have budget. She'll source candidates this week.

**Mobile vs. web design parity.** Stencil is mobile-first but we have a web companion app. Web design has been stale for months because she's been heads-down on mobile. She's worried it's becoming a quality issue.

I told her: web is intentionally lower-priority. We don't have web engineers focused on it. Don't put yourself in a position of designing things that won't ship for 6 months. She agreed.

**Design system maintenance.** Asked if she should formalize the design system more — Figma libraries, component documentation, etc. Said it would take 2 weeks of her time.

I pushed back. The current design system is fine for our scale. We don't need formal documentation until we have 2+ designers. Save the 2 weeks.

**Career conversation.** Lin doesn't bring this up often. This time she did. She asked: am I a "lead designer" or am I about to become a "design manager" or am I going to stay an IC?

Honest answer: unclear right now. She's effectively the head of design but as IC. If we hire another designer (or two), that becomes a meaningful question.

I asked her preference. She said she likes designing more than managing but enjoys mentoring. We agreed: she stays IC-track, mentors the contract designer, and we revisit when we hire a full-time second designer.

## What I brought up

**The investing tab — design progress.** I reviewed her latest mocks. The portfolio-value-vs-performance question we discussed in last 1:1 is resolved well. The auto-invest setup flow is still too long (8 screens). She agreed; cutting to 5.

**Cross-pod design consistency.** Mobile and backend (audit views, internal tools) have drifted. She's been focused on mobile. We agreed: she does a quarterly design audit on internal surfaces, and we accept that the audit views won't get full design treatment until we have more capacity.

**Her compensation.** I told her — without prompting — that she's underpaid relative to lead-designer market rates. We'll adjust post-Series B. She said thanks; she had not asked.

This is the kind of thing I should do more of. Tell people they're being undervalued before they have to ask.

Lin has been quietly building a portfolio of her own work outside Stencil — open-source UI components for fintech apps. I noticed in her GitHub. Not a problem. Not a flight risk signal yet. Worth keeping an eye on.
