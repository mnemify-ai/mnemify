## What's driving the volume issues

- **The market.** Senior engineers aren't churning right now. Companies are holding onto them.
- **Our brand.** We're not a household name. Most candidates have never heard of Stencil before applying.
- **No recruiter.** We've been sourcing through network. Network has limits.

## Marcus's referral bonus proposal

Marcus has been pushing for a $5k referral bonus. He thinks our employees have networks we're underutilizing.

I've been on the fence. Pros: incentivizes employees to think about who they'd love to work with. Cons: referral bonuses can become quota-driven and quality drops.

Decision: implement, but with guardrails:
- $5k for hires that stay > 6 months
- Maximum 3 referrals per employee per year (prevents spray-and-pray)
- Track quality: are referred hires performing at the same level as sourced hires after 90 days?

Aleks signed off. Effective June 1.
