# Senior Backend Engineer — Job Description

**Status:** Active, posted
**Author:** Maya (with Marcus)
**Date:** 2026-05-08
**Hiring manager:** Marcus

## About Stencil

Stencil is a consumer fintech app helping people save and invest in small amounts automatically. We're Series A, ~30 people, based in SF. We've moved $180M+ in customer savings since launch. Investors include Bessemer and Ribbit.

We're hiring our 5th and 6th backend engineers. The team is small enough that you'll have ownership over real systems from day one and visibility into business decisions that affect what you build.

## What you'll do

- Own backend systems for money movement, identity, and investing
- Design and ship distributed systems that handle real money for real customers
- Work closely with mobile, data, and product teams
- Participate in on-call (compensated)
- Write code, review code, mentor mid-level engineers
- Help us hire (we want hiring partners, not just code-writers)

## Stack

- **Languages:** Go (services), Ruby (Rails monolith — being carved up over time), some Python (data)
- **Storage:** Postgres (primary), Snowflake (analytics), Redis (caching)
- **Infrastructure:** AWS (EKS), Datadog, GitHub Actions
- **Money movement:** Custom pipelines + Plaid, Pillar Bank (BaaS), DriveWealth (investing)

## What we're looking for

- 5+ years backend engineering experience
- Has built distributed systems where correctness matters (money movement, healthcare, infrastructure — anywhere real consequences attach to bugs)
- Strong with at least one of Go, Ruby/Rails
- Comfortable with Postgres at scale (knows when an index helps, knows when to denormalize, knows what `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` says)
- Can write and review thoughtful code (good test coverage, defensive programming, mindful of failure modes)
- Has been on call and handled incidents
- Strong communicator (we work async-first, written communication matters)

## Bonus

- Prior fintech experience (sponsor bank dynamics, NACHA basics, KYC flows)
- Has shipped FedRAMP or PCI-compliant systems
- Has used Kubernetes in production (not just demos)
- Has experience with regulated audits

## What we're NOT looking for

- People who only want to work on greenfield. We have real customers and real legacy.
- People who don't enjoy debugging. The job is sometimes 80% debugging.
- People who need formal processes to operate. We're light on process.

## Comp

- **Base salary:** $200-260k depending on level
- **Equity:** Significant. Real meaningful equity at Series A.
- **Benefits:** Standard SF startup package — full health, 401k match, equipment budget, learning budget
- **On-call:** $200/week stipend when on rotation

## Interview process

1. Recruiter screen (30 min) — basics, comp expectations
2. Hiring manager screen with Marcus (45 min) — background, technical chat
3. Technical deep dive (60 min) — pair-programming on a small problem, mostly conversation about design choices
4. System design (60 min) — design a fintech-flavored problem (we'll share the problem ahead)
5. Cross-team panel (60 min) — Nadia (mobile) and Devon (data) chat with you about cross-functional work
6. Maya conversation (45 min) — values, motivations, role-specific
7. Reference checks
8. Offer

About 2-3 weeks from start to finish if calendars cooperate.

## What you won't get from us

- Free lunches every day. We have lunch sometimes, not always.
- Massage chairs. Sorry.
- A title hierarchy with 11 levels. We have 3.
- Marketing pretending we're a "rocket ship." We're a serious company. We don't need the rocket emoji.

## What you will get

- Ownership over real systems that handle real money
- Direct exposure to founders and board
- Time and mentorship to grow
- A team that ships
- Equity that could matter
- Honest feedback in every direction
