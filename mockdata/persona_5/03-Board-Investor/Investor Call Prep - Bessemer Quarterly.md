## Topics + my talking points

### April incident — the full version

What I'd tell the full board: it happened, we fixed it, here's the rebuild.

What I'll tell Ethan: I should have seen this coming. The Sidekiq architecture was fragile and I knew it. We had budget to fix it in February. I prioritized investing feature scoping. That trade-off was wrong. The architectural fix is now in flight and I expect to be done by mid-June. The thing I'm taking from this is: infrastructure debt at our stage compounds faster than I was modeling.

Ethan will push on this. He should. The honest answer is the right answer.

### Investing feature — risk profile

Honest assessment: 60% chance it ships Q3 as planned, 30% chance Q4 slip, 10% chance bigger slip. DriveWealth integration is the critical path. Their team has been responsive but we're dependent on them for things we can't accelerate.

If we slip into Q4, Series B narrative changes from "shipped" to "shipping." Ethan will know how to position that with other firms.

### Series B timing — his read

I want to hear:
- Are the funds I'm thinking about (a16z, Lightspeed, Founders Fund consumer team) the right ones?
- Who's currently hot vs. cold on consumer fintech?
- What's the right post-money expectation? My back-of-envelope is $200-240M. He sees more market data than I do.
- Should we kick off before or after investing launch?

### VPE compensation

I want him aligned that we'll pay top-of-band for the right VPE candidate. Maybe over-band if needed. The cost of a bad VPE hire at our stage is much higher than the cost of paying 10% more for the right one.

Aleks is sympathetic but cost-conscious. Ethan helps if he's bought in.

- The architecture diagram. Save for the next architecture review with him.
- Hiring funnel details. Aleks owns the headcount narrative.
