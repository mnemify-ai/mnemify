# Conflict of Interest Disclosure

**Author:** Carlos Morales
**Status:** Internal disclosure to Reema and David, ahead of publication
**Date:** 2026-05-10

## Why this document exists

Investigative reporters are expected to disclose, to their editors before publication, any relationships, financial interests, or other circumstances that could be characterized as conflicts of interest, even when the characterization would be unfair. The standard at *The Drift* is the standard I respect: disclose what could appear to be a conflict, not only what is. The editor decides what to do with the disclosure.

This document is my disclosure for the Mesa Verde piece. I am submitting it to Reema and David four months before publication. I have not previously made these disclosures because, until recently, several of them did not exist.

## Financial interests

I have no investments in any of the entities discussed in the piece. I have no financial relationships with Andrew Lacher, with Verdant Holdings or its principals, with the Phoenix law firm of Halsey Vincent & Brand, with the Mesa Verde Irrigation District, or with any of the state-level entities or officials discussed.

I have no investments in any agricultural or water-related fund, commodity, or other vehicle. I have, after consulting with the magazine's general counsel in 2023, divested from a small position I had previously held in a publicly-traded agricultural infrastructure REIT. The divestiture occurred before I began this piece. The position was approximately $4,200 at the time of divestiture.

My income, for the past six years, has consisted entirely of my salary from *The Drift*, occasional honoraria from speaking engagements (all disclosed and approved), and royalties from a 2017 book that has no connection to the subject of this piece.

## Personal relationships

I have no personal relationships with anyone discussed in the piece that predate the reporting. I have developed, in the course of the reporting, professional relationships with my sources that are described in detail in the source files. These relationships are professional. They have not, in my judgment, compromised my reporting.

I am aware that the relationship with Helen Fung has been the most extensive and the most personally significant of the source relationships. I have considered whether the depth of the relationship has affected my reporting on her. My conclusion is that it has affected my reporting in the sense that the relationship is the reason the reporting exists; it has not affected my reporting in the sense that the substance of what I have reported is what the evidence supports.

Reema is aware of the depth of the relationship and has, in editorial conversations, monitored my characterization of Helen for indications of over-sympathy. Her conclusion has aligned with mine: the characterization is what the reporting supports.

## Past professional relationships

I have previously written about Andrew Lacher's firm in a single paragraph in a 2019 piece on agricultural investment trends. The mention was neutral and did not name Mr. Lacher personally. I do not consider this a meaningful prior relationship but I am disclosing it.

I have, in the past, interviewed two members of the New Mexico State Water Resources Board for unrelated pieces. Neither of those board members is among the individuals discussed in this piece.

I am personally acquainted, from professional contexts, with three of the photographers who have done significant work on western water issues. None of them is the photographer the piece's companion visual essay is being developed with.

## Family and household connections

My wife, Beatriz Morales, is an art historian. She has read drafts of the piece and offered feedback. Her feedback has improved the piece. She has no professional or financial connections to any of the matters discussed.

My daughter Marina, who is nine, has no connection to any matter discussed in the piece. I mention her only because, in two of the source files (the Sandoval file and the Helen Fung file), the source has asked questions about my family that I have answered. Hortensia Sandoval and Helen Fung both know that I have a wife and a daughter. They do not know more than that.

## Ideological positions

I have, in past published work, written about extractive industries, about private equity, and about the financialization of public goods. The pieces have been factually accurate but have, in their framing, expressed views that could be characterized as critical of private capital's role in these matters.

I am aware that this prior body of work could be used to characterize my reporting on the Mesa Verde matter as ideologically pre-committed. I have considered this characterization. I do not think it is accurate. My prior reporting has been critical because the evidence in those prior cases supported critical conclusions. My current reporting is critical because the evidence in this case supports critical conclusions. The pattern is the pattern of the underlying facts, not of my ideological commitments.

Other reporters with different ideological commitments could, I believe, write the same piece on the same evidence. The piece is constructed to be defensible on its facts, not on its framing.

## Reporting limitations

I have not, in this investigation, been able to interview Andrew Lacher. I have not been able to interview any current member of Verdant Holdings's senior leadership. I have not obtained any internal Verdant Holdings document directly (the materials I have come from court filings, FOIA productions, and source-provided documents whose provenance is established but whose authentication has not been formally tested).

These limitations are noted in the piece. The piece does not claim access it does not have. Readers will know what I was able to do and what I was not able to do.

## Editorial decisions discussed elsewhere

The decision to withhold material relating to the tribal nation's likely federal action is discussed separately, in a dedicated document. The decision was made jointly with Reema and David. It is documented as a decision and not as an undisclosed limitation.

## What I am not aware of

I have not, to my knowledge, omitted any disclosure that should be in this document. If anyone within the magazine becomes aware of a relationship, interest, or circumstance that I have not disclosed and that they consider relevant, I would appreciate being told. I would prefer to disclose now than to be discovered later.

## On record

This document is on record with Reema and David. It will be available to the magazine's general counsel during legal review. It will not be published.

If I am ever asked, in connection with this piece, about conflicts of interest, I will speak to the contents of this document. I will not represent that no conflicts exist; I will represent that the disclosures I have made are complete to the best of my knowledge.
