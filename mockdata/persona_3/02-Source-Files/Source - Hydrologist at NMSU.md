# Source — Dr. Inés Maldonado-Reyes (NMSU Hydrologist)

**Real name:** Dr. Inés Maldonado-Reyes
**Level of attribution:** On the record, full name and title
**Date of first contact:** 2025-07-22
**Date of most recent conversation:** 2026-05-14
**Number of meetings:** Six
**Author of this file:** Carlos Morales

## Background

Professor of hydrology at New Mexico State University. Forty-nine years old. Has been mapping actual water flows in the Mesa Verde watershed and adjacent areas for the past twenty years, as part of an academic research program funded variously by the National Science Foundation, the U.S. Geological Survey, and a private foundation whose grant terms permit independent publication of her findings.

Her published work includes a 2019 paper in *Water Resources Research* that documented, in technical terms, the divergence between paper water-rights records and actual flow data in the region. The paper attracted modest attention in academic circles and no attention from journalists at the time.

I read the paper in November 2024, after Esther mentioned it to me obliquely. I wrote to Dr. Maldonado-Reyes the following month. She agreed to meet.

## How we met

By appointment, in her office at NMSU's main campus in Las Cruces. The first meeting was forty-five minutes and consisted, almost entirely, of her testing whether I understood the technical content of her published work well enough for further conversation to be worthwhile. I passed, narrowly. Subsequent meetings have been longer.

## Agreed terms

On the record from the start. She has explained that her role as an academic is to make her work public and to defend it when challenged. She has no interest in pseudonymous attribution and has, in fact, asked that her university affiliation appear in the piece.

She has, separately, asked that any technical claim attributed to her be reviewed by her before publication. This is standard practice for academic sources and I have agreed.

## What she has provided

- The technical foundation for the piece's central factual claim: that paper water rights in the Mesa Verde watershed have diverged from physical water flows in measurable, documentable ways
- Forty maps, slides, and data visualizations that demonstrate the divergence
- An explanation of why the divergence is consequential, in language that I have been able to use in the piece without flattening the science
- Access to her broader research network, including two graduate students who have done complementary work
- A reading of the state water board's technical capacity — the people, the equipment, the institutional knowledge — that has informed my understanding of how the consolidations passed administrative review

## What she has been careful about

Dr. Maldonado-Reyes has been clear that she will not speculate about the motivations or intent of any individual involved in the consolidations. She will speak about systems, models, and measurable phenomena. She will not speak about Andrew Lacher or Helen Fung or anyone else as people.

I have respected this. The piece does not, and will not, quote her on any matter of personal characterization.

## My assessment

Dr. Maldonado-Reyes is the gold standard of source for an investigation like this. She is publicly identified. She is technically expert. She is willing to be challenged. She has done the underlying work herself. She has no financial interest in the outcome. She has no political grievance.

The piece is much better because she is in it.

## What I have promised her

That her technical claims will be quoted precisely. That I will not paraphrase her in ways that introduce technical inaccuracy. That I will provide her with a draft of the technical sections of the piece, with sufficient time to review and request changes, before publication.

That if her work is subsequently criticized in print or elsewhere, I will not characterize the criticism in a way that misrepresents her response.

That if anyone associated with Verdant Holdings, or any party adverse to the piece, attempts to discredit her professionally as a result of her cooperation with me, I will write about it.
