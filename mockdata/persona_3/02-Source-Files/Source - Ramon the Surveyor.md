# Source — Ramón ("the surveyor")

**Real name:** Ramón Vargas
**Level of attribution as of 2026-05-26:** Deep background; will not appear by name
**Date of first contact:** 2025-04-03
**Date of most recent conversation:** 2026-03-21
**Number of meetings:** Five
**Author of this file:** Carlos Morales

## Background

Sixty-eight years old. Worked as a land surveyor for forty-three years, the last twenty-eight of them on contract with various agencies and private clients in the southwestern United States. Husband of Esther (see separate file). They have been married forty-six years.

Ramón is not, technically, a participant in the events the story documents. He is, however, the person who first noticed, by overlaying his own survey work on the legal water-rights records, that the paper geography and the physical geography of the Mesa Verde Irrigation District had come apart. He told Esther. Esther, eventually, told me.

He is, in other words, the proximate cause of the investigation.

## How we met

Through Esther. After my third meeting with her, in February 2025, she said: "You should talk to my husband. He saw it first."

I went to their house. Ramón was wary at first. He is, by his wife's description and my own observation, a man who prefers his work to his words. He answered my questions for the first hour in monosyllables. He warmed up after Esther brought out a survey map he had made in 1998 and laid it on the table next to a water-rights record from 2019.

He has, since then, become one of the most important sources for the piece, despite — or because of — his unwillingness to be the public face of anything.

## Agreed terms

He will not be named. He will not be quoted directly. He will not be described in any way that allows identification.

His information may be used. He has been clear that this includes the maps he has shown me, the analysis he has done, and the explanation he has provided of how a water-rights certificate physically corresponds (or does not correspond) to actual water in actual canals.

He has agreed, separately, that if the piece references "a surveyor who has spent forty years mapping the region," this characterization is acceptable, provided it does not appear in conjunction with any reference to Esther's husband. I have agreed not to mention his marital relationship to Esther in any context that could lead to identification.

## What he has provided

- The original observation that the paper rights and the physical flows had diverged
- Forty years of survey maps, which I have been allowed to photograph but not publish
- An understanding of how the canals actually work, which I needed in order to write about them accurately
- Names of two other surveyors who have done similar overlay work in adjacent districts (both of whom have declined to be sources, citing professional concerns)

## What he has not provided

He has not provided opinions about the people involved. When I asked him, in our third meeting, what he thought of Andrew Lacher or Helen Fung, he said: "I do not know them. I will not tell you what I think of people I do not know." I respected this and did not press.

He has not provided documents from his own client files, citing professional confidentiality obligations. The survey maps he has shown me are from his personal archive of completed projects, which he has retained for his own reference.

## My assessment of his reliability

Very high. His work is precise. His observations are restrained. He distinguishes between what he has measured and what he has inferred. When I have asked him questions that exceed his expertise, he has said so directly and referred me to others.

The risk with Ramón is not reliability. The risk is that I will lean too heavily on his analysis and the piece will become a story about surveying rather than a story about people. I have been careful, in the drafts, to use his analysis sparingly and to render it in terms that do not require the reader to follow technical reasoning.

## What I have promised him

That he will not be named. That his survey maps will not be published in a form that allows identification of his personal archive. That I will not reveal his marriage to Esther in the published piece.

These are commitments I will honor regardless of any subsequent change in his preference. He is one of the sources for whom the original agreement is the final agreement.

## Status

Active but quiet. He has indicated he has no further material to offer. He has asked that I not contact him again until I have a complete draft of the piece, at which point he is willing to do a final accuracy check on the technical sections.
