# Fact-Check Protocol for Kayla

**From:** Carlos Morales
**To:** Kayla Borowski, Fact-Checker
**Date:** 2026-05-12
**Subject:** Fact-check protocol for Mesa Verde piece

---

Kayla,

You and I have done eight pieces together over the past four years. You know how I work. I want to write down, for this piece specifically, what I am going to need from you and what I am going to give you in return. Some of what I am about to say is what we always do. Some of it is specific to this piece, which is larger and more complex than anything we have worked on together.

**The scope:**

55,000 to 60,000 words of text, plus a companion visual essay with shorter accompanying text. Eight chapters. Approximately 340 documented factual claims that will require source verification. Approximately 47 named individuals. Three jurisdictions. Two languages (some Spanish-language sources). A complex corporate structure that crosses sixteen LLCs and several state lines.

This is, by my count, the largest single piece *The Drift* has run in three years. I want to acknowledge the scope before we get into anything else.

**Timing:**

Fact-check kickoff: August 20, 2026
Fact-check completion: September 18, 2026
That gives us approximately four weeks. I think we will need every day of it.

If you need more time, tell me now, not on September 10. I would rather move publication than rush you.

**What I will give you:**

A complete, structured fact-check file for the piece. The file will be organized chapter by chapter, with every factual assertion enumerated, every source identified, every relevant document attached or referenced.

Specifically, you will receive:

- A canonical chronology (the master chronology document I have maintained throughout reporting; you have seen this)
- A source list with full contact information for every named source, and an encrypted file with information for unnamed sources that I will share with you separately and that you should not keep in any persistent file
- A document binder including all FOIA productions, court filings, property records, and corporate filings I have used
- Annotated source files indicating which claims in the piece each source supports
- Drafts of right-to-respond letters for every person named in the piece, prepared with our outside counsel
- My direct notes from every interview, in encrypted form

**What you will give me:**

A list of every factual assertion in the piece that you have not been able to confirm, with notes on what additional reporting or sourcing would be necessary to confirm it. I will then either obtain the additional sourcing or, where appropriate, alter the assertion.

A list of every claim in the piece that you believe could be made more precise, even if it is technically accurate as written.

A list of every passage where my sourcing is, in your professional judgment, thin enough to warrant additional reinforcement before publication.

**Communication during fact-check:**

Daily, by phone, for at least the first two weeks. I will be available for any number of calls at any time of day. I would prefer that you flag concerns as you encounter them rather than aggregating them into longer reports. Small issues become large issues when they sit unresolved.

I will be in Mexico City for most of August and early September. I will be in New York the week of August 19 through 23, available for in-person work.

**Specific challenges I want to flag:**

1. **The Lacher quote in Chapter 4.** This is the single most consequential factual claim in the piece. The quote is attributed to a single unnamed source. I have corroborated it with two other unnamed sources who have not heard the exact quote but who confirm the substance. I have asked Helen Fung whether she ever heard Mr. Lacher discuss the question of seller understanding; she has declined to answer on confidentiality grounds. I would like you to challenge me on this quote until you are satisfied with my level of confidence. I am willing to defend it. I want you to make me defend it.

2. **The Sandoval material.** The material about Manuel Sandoval's cognitive decline is sourced to Hortensia Sandoval and to her daughter Yolanda. Hortensia has consented to its use. I want you to confirm Hortensia's consent independently, in your own voice, before publication. Do not take my word for it. Speak to her yourself. She is expecting your call.

3. **The 2017 meeting at Verdant Holdings.** Verdant Former Employee 2 has provided an account of this meeting. I have asked Employees 1 and 3 to confirm or qualify the account. They have done so, in different language. I would like you to read all three accounts and confirm that, in your judgment, the meeting can be described as the piece describes it.

4. **The redacted federal records.** The piece characterizes the pattern of federal redactions as suggesting internal concern. The characterization is mine; it is supported by the FOIA appeal record and by the federal source's general framing. The characterization is, however, a characterization rather than a documented fact. I want you to be comfortable with the language I am using before we publish.

5. **The tribal nation material.** This is the area where the piece will receive the most scrutiny from readers who specialize in Indigenous water rights. The chapter is deliberately short and deliberately acknowledges its own limits. I want your read on whether the acknowledgments are sufficient to head off the criticism that we are not telling the full story. They will not be fully sufficient — we are not telling the full story — but I want your judgment on whether the framing is adequate to the editorial decision we have made.

**A larger note:**

I am, at this stage of my career, more aware than I have ever been that what I publish will be tested in ways I cannot fully anticipate. The piece will be read by attorneys for Verdant Holdings. It will be read by state water officials who may have to explain their decisions. It will be read by colleagues at other publications who will look for any error they can use to challenge our reporting. It will be read by future Esthers — by the people, somewhere else, who have noticed that something similar is happening in their watershed and are deciding whether to come forward.

I am asking you, in this fact-check, to be the standard against which all of those readings will be measured. I will not be insulted by your challenges. I will be insulted by their absence.

— Carlos
