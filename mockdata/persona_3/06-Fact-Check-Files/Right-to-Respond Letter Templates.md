# Right-to-Respond Letter Templates

**Author:** Carlos Morales, with review by *The Drift*'s outside counsel
**Status:** Templates for use during pre-publication notification
**Date:** 2026-05-25

## Why these exist

Every named individual or entity that is characterized in the piece will receive, before publication, a letter notifying them of the specific characterizations and inviting them to respond. The letters are not, despite their name, simply an invitation. They are a professional obligation. They are also the basis on which we will defend the piece if it is challenged.

The letters need to be specific enough that the recipient cannot later claim they were not given adequate notice of what we intended to publish. They need to be general enough that they do not reveal information about our sources or our reporting methods.

This document contains the templates we will use, with notes on customization per recipient.

## Template 1 — For Andrew Lacher and Verdant Holdings

The letter to Mr. Lacher and to the firm will be substantially similar. Two letters will be sent — one to Mr. Lacher personally, one to the firm through its registered agent. The same content; the formal structure differs.

---

[Letterhead: *The Drift*]

[Date]

[Recipient name and address]

Re: Upcoming publication regarding Verdant Holdings and its acquisitions in the Mesa Verde Irrigation District

Dear [Recipient],

*The Drift* magazine is preparing to publish an investigative piece concerning the acquisition by Verdant Holdings of water-rights certificates in the Mesa Verde Irrigation District during the period 2014 through 2019, and the consolidation of those certificates that received state administrative approval in 2022. I am the author of the piece. Reema Patel, our deputy editor, oversees the editorial process.

In keeping with our professional obligations, I am writing to provide you advance notice of the specific characterizations that will appear in the piece. I invite your response. Any response you provide by [date] will be considered for inclusion in the published piece.

The piece will report the following:

1. That Verdant Holdings, through subsidiary entities, acquired 47 parcels of ranchland in three New Mexico counties between 2014 and 2017, for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $14.3 million.

2. That the water-rights certificates associated with these parcels were assigned, at the time of each acquisition, to separate corporate entities under common control, in a structure that did not require disclosure to the Mesa Verde Irrigation District.

3. That the consolidated water-rights portfolio was formally recognized by the district in 2019 and subsequently ratified by the New Mexico State Water Resources Board in 2022.

4. That the consolidated portfolio's current value, based on lease arrangements documented in public court filings, is between $200 million and $400 million.

5. That the strategy described above was designed by you personally, and executed by counsel including Helen Fung of the Phoenix law firm Halsey Vincent & Brand.

6. That you understood, at the time of the acquisitions, that the sellers of the underlying water rights did not have access to information sufficient to evaluate the assets they were transferring.

The piece will further include a direct quotation attributed to you concerning the asymmetry of information between buyer and seller. The quotation has been provided to us by an individual who was present at a strategic meeting at Verdant Holdings in spring 2017. The quotation, as it will appear in the piece, is:

"They understand the value of the price. They have not been asked to understand the value of the asset. That is a different question. The asset is structured in such a way that they will not be asked."

We invite your response to any or all of the characterizations above, including the quotation. We will publish any response you provide that meets our editorial standards, including responses provided by your counsel.

Please direct responses to me at the address below, or to our general counsel at [counsel address] if you prefer.

We anticipate publication on [date]. Responses received by [date] will be considered for inclusion. Responses received after [date] may be included in subsequent editorial coverage as appropriate.

Sincerely,

Carlos Morales
*The Drift*

---

**Notes on this template:**

The phrase "designed by you personally" is the phrase our outside counsel has scrutinized most carefully. The phrase is supported by source testimony but not by documentary evidence in the strict sense. We have concluded that the support is sufficient for publication, given the corroboration from multiple sources.

The inclusion of the direct quotation in the letter is unusual. The decision to include it was made deliberately. By providing the exact language of the quotation in advance, we eliminate any later claim that Mr. Lacher was not given adequate notice of what was attributed to him. He will have approximately three weeks to respond, including the option to deny making the statement, to provide context, or to remain silent. Each choice is open to him.

The piece will name two officials in the New Mexico Governor's Office whose role in the consolidation matter is documented in communications I have obtained. The right-to-respond letters to them are the most legally consequential letters in the entire process.

The letters will be specific about the communications cited and about the characterizations the piece will make of their actions. The letters will be sent at least four weeks before publication. The letters will be sent to their official addresses and, separately, to any counsel of record.

The letters do not, and will not, identify how the communications came to be in my possession.

## What is not in this document

The letters to the unnamed sources are different in kind and are not templated here. Those communications will be made privately, in advance of publication, to confirm the language being used to describe them and to give them the opportunity to ask for stricter attribution. Several have already indicated they may wish to upgrade to a more permissive level of attribution if certain conditions are met. Those negotiations are ongoing.

## Why all of this

Twenty-one years of investigative reporting has taught me that the right-to-respond process is not a defensive tactic. It is the moment when the reporting becomes most morally weighted. The people who receive these letters are people who have done specific things. They are being asked to explain those things. Their responses, or their refusals to respond, will be part of the published record.

I have, in my career, received responses that have changed a piece. I have received responses that have changed my own understanding of what I was reporting. I have received responses that have, occasionally, caused me to alter the central claims of a piece because the response provided information I had not previously had access to.

I am open to all of these possibilities for this piece. I do not expect them. I am open to them.
